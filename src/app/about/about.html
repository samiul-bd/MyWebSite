My Journey: From Visual Basic to Enterprise Architecture

My path into software engineering didn't begin in a traditional Computer Science classroom. It started years ago with an offline Visual Basic CD, a borrowed book, and a fascination for how things work under the hood. Even without an internet connection at the time, my curiosity drove me to reverse-engineer code and build a fully functional 2D racing game from scratch.

Due to the strict academic track systems in Bangladesh, pursuing a STEM degree wasn't an option for a Business Studies student. I went on to complete my graduation in Accounting, where I developed a deep understanding of financial structures, corporate operations, and complex business logic.

However, my passion for programming never faded. To turn this lifelong hobby into a professional career, I secured the prestigious IsDB-BISEW IT Scholarship. Through this rigorous, 788-hour hands-on training program, I mastered enterprise-level software development, focusing heavily on cross-platform apps using C#, ASP.NET Core, SQL Server, Angular, and React.

Today, I bridge the gap between business needs and technical execution. Coming from an accounting background, I don't just write code; I understand the business logic behind the software. Whether it's architecting a scalable domain-driven backend, optimizing complex SQL queries, or developing seamless Single Page Applications (SPAs), my goal is to build fault-tolerant, high-performance systems that solve real-world problems.
